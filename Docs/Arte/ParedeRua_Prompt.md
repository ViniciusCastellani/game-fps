# Parede com pichacao do derby

Gerada com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` (sem CLI).

Textura: `Assets/DerbySegundoTempo/Cenarios/Rua/Texturas/Parede_Derby_Corinthians_Palmeiras.png`.

Destino: material exclusivo de `BloqueioDaRua_70` na cena `Rua_TPS`. A aplicacao preserva a transformacao, a malha e o BoxCollider do objeto.

## Prompt usado

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: opaque game environment wall base-color texture, for a Unity street scene. Generate a single production-ready raster image, approximately 2048 x 1296 pixels, aspect ratio 36:22.8.
Primary request: a huge weathered Brazilian concrete and plaster wall with football fan graffiti about CORINTHIANS and PALMEIRAS.
Composition: perfectly straight-on orthographic flat wall surface, edge-to-edge wall texture. No visible wall silhouette or outside scene. The complete image must be the wall surface. This will map once across a wall 36 metres wide and 22.8 metres high.
Materials: aged gray-beige concrete plaster, subtle large construction panel seams, small cracks, peeling patches, pores, grime and rain streaks; muted and slightly stylized for a low-poly third-person game, detailed enough to read as real masonry. Even diffuse illumination suitable for albedo; no directional shadows or photographic perspective.
Graffiti: spontaneous Brazilian street pichação and spray paint, hand-painted angular letters, rough edges, overspray, drips and overlapping older faded marks. Football rivalry theme in black, white and green. The two team names must be the only large readable words and must be spelled exactly "CORINTHIANS" and "PALMEIRAS". Black-and-white Corinthians lettering with a small hand-sprayed anchor/shield motif; green-and-white Palmeiras lettering with a small circular P crest motif. Both clubs should be clearly represented with balanced visual prominence.
Layout: keep the upper half mostly aged concrete with a few faded small abstract tags. Concentrate the main readable names and their simple club motifs in a broad graffiti band across the lower half, keeping about 8 percent margin from the bottom and side edges so the text is not clipped. Paint belongs directly on the wall; no billboards or framed posters.
Constraints: opaque image, flat normal-facing wall, no sky, ground, road, people, buildings, camera angle, watermark, interface, frames, or extra slogans. No hostile or discriminatory imagery. No 3D mockup.
```
